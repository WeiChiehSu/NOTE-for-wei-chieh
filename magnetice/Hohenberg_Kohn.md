# Hohenberg–Kohn theorems: from the ground-state density to the variational principle

這份筆記的主線是：DFT 不直接把多體波函數 $\Psi$ 當成基本變數，而是希望使用電子密度 $n(\mathbf r)$ 描述基態。第一 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理說明「基態密度可以決定外部勢，因此也能決定 Hamiltonian 與基態物理」；第二 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理則說明「真正的基態密度會讓能量泛函取得最小值」。

The main idea of these notes is that DFT does not use the many-electron wave function $\Psi$ as the basic variable. Instead, it tries to describe the ground state by the electron density $n(\mathbf r)$. The first Hohenberg–Kohn theorem explains why the ground-state density determines the external potential, and therefore the Hamiltonian and the ground-state physics. The second theorem explains why the true ground-state density minimizes the energy functional.

---

## 1. Electron density as the basic variable

對一個 $N$-electron system，電子密度可以寫成 density operator 的 expectation value：

For an $N$-electron system, the electron density can be written as the expectation value of the density operator:

$$ n(\mathbf r)=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\sum_{i=1}^{N}\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_i)\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 這個式子的意思是：在位置 $\mathbf r$ 上，把每一個電子 $i$ 出現在該位置的 contribution 全部加起來。  This expression means that at the position $\mathbf r$, we add the contribution from every electron $i$ that can appear at that position.  如果把多體波函數寫成 $\Psi(\mathbf r,\mathbf r_2,\ldots,\mathbf r_N)$，那麼也可以把其他 $N-1$ 個電子的位置全部積分掉：  If the many-electron wave function is written as $\Psi(\mathbf r,\mathbf r_2,\ldots,\mathbf r_N)$, we can integrate out the coordinates of the other $N-1$ electrons: $$ n(\mathbf r)=N\int d^{3}\mathbf r_2\cdots d^{3}\mathbf r_N\,\lvert\Psi(\mathbf r,\mathbf r_2,\ldots,\mathbf r_N)\rvert^{2}. $$ 前面的 factor $N$ 是因為任意一個電子都可以出現在 $\mathbf r$。  The factor $N$ appears because any one of the $N$ electrons can be the electron located at $\mathbf r$.  把 density 對所有空間積分，應該得到總電子數：  Integrating the density over all space gives the total number of electrons: $$ \int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)=N. $$ 所以 $n(\mathbf r)$ 不是單一電子的 probability density，而是「每單位體積的電子數」。  Therefore, $n(\mathbf r)$ is not the probability density of one electron. It is the number of electrons per unit volume.  ---  ## 2. The external potential can be written with the density  多電子系統中的外部勢 operator 是每一個電子受到的外部勢總和：  The external-potential operator of a many-electron system is the sum of the external potential acting on every electron: $$ \hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}V(\mathbf r_i). $$ 它的 expectation value 是  Its expectation value is $$ \left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\sum_{i=1}^{N}V(\mathbf r_i)\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 利用 electron density 的定義，可以把這個 expectation value 改寫成  Using the definition of the electron density, this expectation value can be rewritten as $$ \left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle=\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r). $$ 這一步很重要，因為它顯示至少 external-potential energy 可以直接寫成 density 的形式，而不必一直保留完整的 many-electron wave function。  This step is important because it shows that the external-potential energy can be written directly in terms of the density, without keeping the full many-electron wave function explicitly.  ---  # 3. First Hohenberg–Kohn theorem  第一 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理的核心內容是：  The central statement of the first Hohenberg–Kohn theorem is:  基態電子密度 $n_0(\mathbf r)$ 可以唯一決定外部勢 $V(\mathbf r)$，最多只差一個加法常數 $C$。  The ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$ uniquely determines the external potential $V(\mathbf r)$, up to an additive constant $C$.  可以把關係寫成  The logical relation is $$ n_0(\mathbf r)\longrightarrow V(\mathbf r)+C. $$ 為什麼這很重要？因為一旦 $V(\mathbf r)$ 被決定，Hamiltonian 就被決定；Hamiltonian 決定 ground-state wave function；wave function 再決定所有 ground-state observables。  Why is this important? Once $V(\mathbf r)$ is determined, the Hamiltonian is determined. The Hamiltonian determines the ground-state wave function, and the ground-state wave function determines all ground-state observables.  因此可以寫成  Therefore, $$ V(\mathbf r)\longrightarrow\hat H\longrightarrow\Psi_0\longrightarrow\text{ground-state physics}. $$ 所以如果 $n_0(\mathbf r)$ 能決定 $V(\mathbf r)$，那麼 ground-state physics 最後也可以視為 density 的 functional。  Therefore, if $n_0(\mathbf r)$ determines $V(\mathbf r)$, then all ground-state physics can ultimately be regarded as a functional of the density.  ---  ## 4. Why a proof by contradiction is used  手寫筆記用反證法證明第一定理。  The handwritten notes prove the first theorem by contradiction.  假設存在兩個不同的外部勢  Assume that there are two different external potentials $$ V(\mathbf r)\neq V'(\mathbf r), $$ 而且它們不是只差一個常數，但卻產生完全相同的 ground-state density：  and that they do not differ only by a constant, but nevertheless produce exactly the same ground-state density: $$ n(\mathbf r)=n'(\mathbf r). $$ 我們要證明這個假設會產生矛盾。  We will show that this assumption produces a contradiction.  對第一個系統，  For the first system, $$ \hat H=\hat T+\hat V_{ee}+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}, $$ 其中  with $$ \hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}V(\mathbf r_i). $$ 令它的 ground state 和 ground-state energy 分別為  Let its ground state and ground-state energy be $$ \hat H\Psi=E\Psi. $$ 對第二個系統，  For the second system, $$ \hat H'=\hat T+\hat V_{ee}+\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}, $$ 其中  with $$ \hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}V'(\mathbf r_i), $$ 並且  and $$ \hat H'\Psi'=E'\Psi'. $$ 兩個 Hamiltonian 的 kinetic term 和 electron-electron interaction 相同，差別只在 external potential。  The two Hamiltonians have the same kinetic term and electron-electron interaction. They differ only in the external potential.  ---  ## 5. Apply the variational theorem to the first system  對 $\hat H$ 而言，$\Psi$ 是真正的 ground state。  For $\hat H$, the wave function $\Psi$ is the true ground state.  因此把另一個系統的 $\Psi'$ 當成 trial wave function 時，variational theorem 告訴我們：  If we use $\Psi'$, the ground state of the other system, as a trial wave function, the variational theorem gives $$ E<\left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle. $$ 這裡使用嚴格小於號，是因為假設兩個 external potentials 真正不同，因此它們的 ground states 也不是同一個 ground state。  The strict inequality is used here because the two external potentials are assumed to be genuinely different, so the two ground states are not the same ground state.  把 $\hat H$ 改寫成  Rewrite $\hat H$ as $$ \hat H=\hat H'+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}. $$ 所以  Therefore, $$ \left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle=\left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat H'\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle+\left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle. $$ 由於 $\Psi'$ 是 $\hat H'$ 的 ground state，  Because $\Psi'$ is the ground state of $\hat H'$, $$ \left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat H'\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle=E'. $$ 因此  Thus, $$ E<E'+\left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle. $$ 再利用 external potential expectation value 可以寫成 density integral：  Using the density representation of the external-potential expectation value, $$ \left\langle\Psi'\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi'\right\rangle=\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n'(\mathbf r)\left[V(\mathbf r)-V'(\mathbf r)\right]. $$ 所以  Therefore, $$ E<E'+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n'(\mathbf r)\left[V(\mathbf r)-V'(\mathbf r)\right]. $$ 現在使用我們反證法的假設  Now use the assumption of the proof by contradiction, $$ n'(\mathbf r)=n(\mathbf r). $$ 得到第一個 inequality：  We obtain the first inequality: $$ E<E'+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V(\mathbf r)-V'(\mathbf r)\right]. $$ ---  ## 6. Exchange the two systems  接著完全交換兩個系統的角色。  Now exchange the roles of the two systems.  對 $\hat H'$ 而言，$\Psi'$ 是真正的 ground state，而 $\Psi$ 可以拿來當 trial wave function。  For $\hat H'$, $\Psi'$ is the true ground state, while $\Psi$ can be used as a trial wave function.  因此  Therefore, $$ E'<\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat H'\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 把 $\hat H'$ 寫成  Write $\hat H'$ as $$ \hat H'=\hat H+\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}. $$ 所以  Thus, $$ \left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat H'\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle=E+\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}-\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 用 density 改寫：  Using the density representation, $$ E'<E+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V'(\mathbf r)-V(\mathbf r)\right]. $$ 這是第二個 inequality。  This is the second inequality.  ---  ## 7. Add the two inequalities and obtain a contradiction  現在把兩個 inequality 相加：  Now add the two inequalities: $$ E+E'<E'+E+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V(\mathbf r)-V'(\mathbf r)\right]+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V'(\mathbf r)-V(\mathbf r)\right]. $$ 兩個積分完全抵消：  The two integrals cancel exactly: $$ \int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V(\mathbf r)-V'(\mathbf r)\right]+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)\left[V'(\mathbf r)-V(\mathbf r)\right]=0. $$ 所以得到  Therefore, $$ E+E'<E'+E. $$ 這是不可能成立的，因此原本的假設錯誤。  This is impossible, so the original assumption is false.  也就是說，兩個真正不同的 external potentials 不可能有完全相同的 ground-state density。  Therefore, two genuinely different external potentials cannot produce exactly the same ground-state density.  這就是第一 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理的反證核心。  This is the core contradiction in the proof of the first Hohenberg–Kohn theorem.  ---  ## 8. Why the external potential is unique only up to a constant  第一定理不是說 $V(\mathbf r)$ 完全唯一，而是說最多可以差一個 constant。  The theorem does not say that $V(\mathbf r)$ is absolutely unique. Two external potentials may differ by an additive constant.  假設  Suppose $$ V'(\mathbf r)=V(\mathbf r)+C. $$ 那麼 many-electron external-potential operator 變成  Then the many-electron external-potential operator becomes $$ \hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[V(\mathbf r_i)+C\right]. $$ 把總和拆開：  Separate the sum: $$ \hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}V(\mathbf r_i)+\sum_{i=1}^{N}C. $$ 因此  Therefore, $$ \hat V'_{\mathrm{ext}}=\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}+NC. $$ 所以 Hamiltonian 只是整體平移：  Thus, the Hamiltonian is shifted only by an overall constant: $$ \hat H'=\hat H+NC. $$ 如果  If $$ \hat H\Psi=E\Psi, $$ 那麼  then $$ \hat H'\Psi=(\hat H+NC)\Psi=(E+NC)\Psi. $$ 因此 wave function 完全不變，只有 energy 整體平移：  Therefore, the wave function is unchanged and only the energy is shifted: $$ E'=E+NC. $$ 因為 density 是由 wave function 決定，所以 density 也完全不變。  Because the density is determined by the wave function, the density is also unchanged.  這就是為什麼第一 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理只能決定 $V(\mathbf r)$ 到一個 additive constant。  This is why the first Hohenberg–Kohn theorem determines $V(\mathbf r)$ only up to an additive constant.  ---  ## 9. Consequence of the first theorem: ground-state quantities are density functionals  第一定理建立了  The first theorem establishes $$ n_0(\mathbf r)\longrightarrow V(\mathbf r)\longrightarrow\hat H\longrightarrow\Psi_0. $$ 因此任何 ground-state observable $A$ 都可以寫成 ground-state density 的 functional：  Therefore, any ground-state observable $A$ can be written as a functional of the ground-state density: $$ A=A[n_0]. $$ 對 ground-state energy，  For the ground-state energy, $$ E_0=\left\langle\Psi_0\left\vert\hat T+\hat V_{ee}\right\vert\Psi_0\right\rangle+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n_0(\mathbf r). $$ 因為第一定理表示 $\Psi_0$ 可以視為 $n_0$ 的 functional，  Because the first theorem allows us to regard $\Psi_0$ as a functional of $n_0$, $$ \Psi_0=\Psi[n_0]. $$ 所以 internal part 也可以寫成 density functional：  The internal part can therefore also be written as a density functional: $$ F[n_0]=\left\langle\Psi[n_0]\left\vert\hat T+\hat V_{ee}\right\vert\Psi[n_0]\right\rangle. $$ 因此  Thus, $$ E_0=F[n_0]+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n_0(\mathbf r). $$ 手寫筆記也把這個 internal functional 分成 kinetic 和 electron-electron interaction：  The handwritten notes also separate this internal functional into kinetic and electron-electron interaction parts: $$ F[n]=T[n]+V_{ee}[n]. $$ 所以  Therefore, $$ E_V[n]=T[n]+V_{ee}[n]+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r). $$ ---  # 10. Second Hohenberg–Kohn theorem  第二 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理告訴我們：  The second Hohenberg–Kohn theorem states that $$ E_V[n]\geq E_V[n_0]=E_0. $$ 意思是：只要拿一個合法的 trial density $n(\mathbf r)$ 去計算 energy functional，它的能量就不可能比真正 ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$ 的能量更低。  This means that for any admissible trial density $n(\mathbf r)$, the value of the energy functional cannot be lower than the energy obtained from the true ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$.  這其實是把 quantum-mechanical wave-function variational principle 轉成 density variational principle。  This is the wave-function variational principle rewritten as a density variational principle.  ---  ## 11. Start from the ordinary quantum variational principle  對固定的 external potential $V(\mathbf r)$，Hamiltonian 是  For a fixed external potential $V(\mathbf r)$, the Hamiltonian is $$ \hat H=\hat T+\hat V_{ee}+\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}. $$ 真正 ground state 滿足  The true ground state satisfies $$ \hat H\Psi_0=E_0\Psi_0. $$ 對任何 normalized trial wave function $\Psi$，variational theorem 給出  For any normalized trial wave function $\Psi$, the variational theorem gives $$ \left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle\geq E_0. $$ 這是第二 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理的起點。  This is the starting point of the second Hohenberg–Kohn theorem.  ---  ## 12. Replace the trial wave function by a trial density  現在考慮一個 trial density $n(\mathbf r)$。  Now consider a trial density $n(\mathbf r)$.  它至少要滿足電子密度的基本條件：  It must at least satisfy the basic conditions for an electron density: $$ n(\mathbf r)\geq0, $$ 以及 electron-number conservation：  and conservation of the total electron number: $$ \int d^{3}\mathbf r\,n(\mathbf r)=N. $$ 這個 trial density 對應到某個 trial wave function $\Psi$，使得  This trial density corresponds to a trial wave function $\Psi$ such that $$ n(\mathbf r)=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\sum_{i=1}^{N}\delta(\mathbf r-\mathbf r_i)\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 第一 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理讓我們可以把 internal energy 視為 density functional，因此  The first Hohenberg–Kohn theorem allows us to regard the internal energy as a functional of the density, so $$ E_V[n]=T[n]+V_{ee}[n]+\int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r). $$ 對應到 trial wave function，  For the corresponding trial wave function, $$ T[n]+V_{ee}[n]=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat T+\hat V_{ee}\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 而 external term 是  The external term is $$ \int d^{3}\mathbf r\,V(\mathbf r)n(\mathbf r)=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat V_{\mathrm{ext}}\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ 所以整個 functional 就是  Therefore, the full functional is $$ E_V[n]=\left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle. $$ ---  ## 13. Apply the variational theorem again  由 quantum-mechanical variational theorem，  From the quantum-mechanical variational theorem, $$ \left\langle\Psi\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi\right\rangle\geq\left\langle\Psi_0\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi_0\right\rangle. $$ 右邊就是 ground-state energy：  The right-hand side is the ground-state energy: $$ \left\langle\Psi_0\left\vert\hat H\right\vert\Psi_0\right\rangle=E_0. $$ 因此  Therefore, $$ E_V[n]\geq E_0. $$ 而真正 ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$ 對應到 $\Psi_0$，所以  For the true ground-state density $n_0(\mathbf r)$, which corresponds to $\Psi_0$, $$ E_V[n_0]=E_0. $$ 最後得到  Hence, $$ E_V[n]\geq E_V[n_0]=E_0. $$ 這就是第二 Hohenberg–Kohn 定理。  This is the second Hohenberg–Kohn theorem.  ---  ## 14. Why the second theorem is useful  第一定理告訴我們 density 足以決定 ground-state physics，但它沒有直接告訴我們怎麼找 $n_0(\mathbf r)$。  The first theorem tells us that the density is sufficient to determine the ground-state physics, but it does not directly tell us how to find $n_0(\mathbf r)$.  第二定理補上這一步：  The second theorem supplies this missing step: $$ n_0(\mathbf r)=\text{the density that minimizes }E_V[n]. $$ 因此 DFT 的 ground-state problem 可以從  Therefore, the DFT ground-state problem can be changed from $$ \text{find the many-electron ground-state wave function }\Psi_0 $$ 轉成  into $$ \text{minimize the energy functional with respect to }n(\mathbf r). $$

這就是手寫筆記中「wave-function variational theorem $\rightarrow$ electron-density variational theorem」的意思。

This is the meaning of the handwritten note that changes the wave-function variational theorem into an electron-density variational theorem.

## 15. Logic of the whole Hohenberg–Kohn argument

這份筆記的完整邏輯可以整理為：

The full logic of the notes is:

1. 用 $n(\mathbf r)$ 描述電子在空間中的分布，並滿足 $\int n=N$。
2. external-potential energy 可以直接寫成 $\int Vn$。
3. 假設兩個不同的 external potentials 產生同一個 ground-state density。
4. 對第一個 Hamiltonian 使用第二個系統的 ground state 作 trial state。
5. 再交換兩個系統，得到第二個 variational inequality。
6. 把兩個 inequality 相加，得到 $E+E'<E'+E$ 的矛盾。
7. 所以 ground-state density 唯一決定 external potential，最多只差 constant。
8. 因為 $V\rightarrow\hat H\rightarrow\Psi_0$，所有 ground-state observables 都可以視為 $n_0$ 的 functional。
9. 再用 ordinary variational theorem 對任意 trial density 對應的 trial wave function。
10. 得到 $E_V[n]\geq E_V[n_0]=E_0$。
11. 所以真正的 ground-state density 就是使 energy functional 最小的 density。
